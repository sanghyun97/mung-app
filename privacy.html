Mung (멍) Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Mung does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • Your settings (such as clock style and auto-switch time) are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions: please open an issue at github.com/sanghyun97/mung-app/issues.
